Retail Sales Data Analysis & Dashboard


📌Project Overview

This project involves end-to-end analysis of 1,000 retail transactions to uncover sales trends, customer segmentation patterns, and product performance insights. An interactive dashboard was developed using Google Sheets to support data-driven decision-making.


🔍 Key Insights

• Revenue is evenly distributed across product categories (Electronics-34.4%, Beauty-31.5%, Clothing-34.1%).

• Female customers contributed 51.1% of the majority sales.

• Mature & Adult age group showed the highest revenue contribution.

• May recorded the highest monthly sales, while the sales significatly drops in September.
